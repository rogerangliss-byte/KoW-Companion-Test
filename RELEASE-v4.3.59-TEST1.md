## Future Officer Targets & Saved Planning Scenarios
- New Planner card for reusable future-Officer targets.
- Select Next, October, January, April or July release session.
- Select 1 or 2 projected Officers.
- Name and save planning scenarios locally.
- Calculates established Legendary requirements:
  - 1 Officer: 1,600 badges, 98,000 Star value, 199,646,700 XP.
  - 2 Officers: 3,200 badges, 196,000 Star value, 399,293,400 XP.
- Shows current readiness and Badge / Star / XP shortfalls.
- Load or delete saved scenarios.
- Preview-only: does not change Inventory.
- Existing v4.3.58 Advanced Planner remains available below the new card.
