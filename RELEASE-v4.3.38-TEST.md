# KoW Companion v4.3.38 TEST

## MAX Officer
MAX Officer saves the Officer profile first, then recalculates and refreshes the visible summaries so a manual browser refresh should no longer be required.

## Legendary Officer Badge requirement
A single Legendary Officer requires 1,600 badges to MAX.
If a plan contains 10 separate Legendary Officers, the combined total is 16,000; each individual Officer still requires 1,600.

## Original Legendary Officers
Original Legendary Officers cannot use ORV or SRV.
They may use:
- their specific Officer Badges
- Universal Legendary Badges

## S2 onward
Seasonal Legendary Officers from S2 onward can use ORV and SRV according to their Officer-specific costs.

## Legendary Officer Badge Selection Chests
Each Selection Chest can be used as either:
- 1 Universal Legendary Badge, or
- 600 ORV

There is no 10-badge chest resource.
